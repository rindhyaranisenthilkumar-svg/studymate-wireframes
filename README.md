
# StudyMate – Mobile App Wireframes

Low-fidelity wireframes for a study planner app that helps students manage tasks, deadlines and schedules.

**Figma:** [View Figma file](https://www.figma.com/design/Mj9LnuC7xfq44DekX5wP7I/studymate-wireframes?node-id=0-1&t=mJqDyIoqVzH8jxpw-1)

## Research
Looked at common problems students face when planning their studies:
- Tasks and deadlines are scattered across notes, chats and paper
- Deadlines are remembered too late
- Students want a simple daily plan and a way to see progress

## Persona
**Priya, 20, college student.** She has five subjects and weekly deadlines. She forgets due dates and wants one screen showing what to do today.

## User Flow
![User Flow](user-flow.png)

Open app → Home → View today's tasks → Mark task done → Check Progress Overview → Open Calendar/Schedule
